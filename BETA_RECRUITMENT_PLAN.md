## Important Reminders

1. **Personal > Scale:** One thoughtful email to someone you know beats 100 cold tweets. Start warm.

2. **Be authentic:** "I built this because I struggled with these problems" >> "You need to use my product"

3. **Show, don't tell:** Get them on a call and screen-share. Seeing it beats describing it.

4. **Listen more than pitch:** Ask questions. What do they like? What sucks? Take notes.

5. **Respect their time:** If they say no, say thanks and move on. No sales pressure.

6. **Document everything:** Every call, every piece of feedback, every concern. Use for product roadmap.
