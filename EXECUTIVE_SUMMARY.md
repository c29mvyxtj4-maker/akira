## How We Get There: 3-Pillar Strategy

### Pillar 1: Customer Acquisition (15-20 signups/month by month 12)

**Tactics:**
- **Product Hunt Launch** (Month 3/October) — Target 8-10 PH-sourced customers
- **Organic Growth** (10-15 signups/month) — SEO, Twitter, referrals, community
- **Network Effect** — Referral program incentivizes existing customers to bring friends

**Investment:** Minimal ($150 CAC) — relying on founder network + organic

### Pillar 2: Healthy Retention (3% monthly churn)

**Tactics:**
- **Strong Onboarding** — 2-min video, interactive tour, email sequence
- **Customer Success** — Monthly check-ins, feature requests tracked, NPS surveys
- **Sticky Features** — Financial data, invoices = high switching cost
- **Continuous Iteration** — Ship features based on customer feedback

**Target:** 90%+ monthly retention by month 6

### Pillar 3: Sustainable Unit Economics (All tiers >3:1 LTV:CAC)

**Tier Analysis:**

| Tier | LTV:CAC | Payback | Health |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Starter** ($29/mo) | 5.4:1 | 3.7 mo | ✅ Good |
| **Professional** ($99/mo) | 19.2:1 | 1.6 mo | ✅ Exceptional |
| **Enterprise** ($500/mo) | 34.9:1 | 1.0 mo | ✅ Exceptional |

**Insight:** Professional tier is the most efficient engine. Focus 60% of sales efforts here.

## Key Risks & Mitigation

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| **Slow customer acquisition** (<10/mo) | Misses $50K ARR | Product Hunt campaign, SEO, content |
| **High churn (>5%)** | Every 1% = -$12K ARR | Customer interviews, feature prioritization |
| **Operating cost increase** | Break-even delayed 2-3 mo | Control hiring, leverage managed services |
| **Product delays** | Feature gaps vs competitors | Prioritize top 3 features, monthly shipping |
| **Market saturation** | Commoditization risk | Build moat (API, integrations, community) |

**Overall Risk Level:** ✅ **MODERATE** (manageable with focus)

## Funding Implications

### Scenario 1: Bootstrap (Realistic) ✅ **Recommended**

- **Initial Capital:** $20K+ savings (12-month runway)
- **Break-even:** Month 6-7 → self-sustaining
- **Equity Given:** 0% (founder keeps 100%)
- **Timeline to Revenue:** Immediate (first customers = first cash)

**Pros:**
- No dilution, full control
- Lean discipline enforced
- Faster decision-making
- No investor board meetings

**Cons:**
- Limited ability to spend on marketing/hiring
- Slower growth than venture-backed competitors
- Personal financial pressure

### Scenario 2: Seed Funding (Month 3+)

- **Raise:** $250K-500K after Product Hunt validation
- **Use:** Hire marketer, accelerate customer acquisition
- **Growth impact:** 2-3x signups/month → $300K+ ARR by year-end
- **Equity:** 10-20% dilution

**Pros:**
- Faster scaling, hire team earlier
- Compete with venture-backed alternatives
- Personal runway extended

**Cons:**
- Equity dilution, investor governance
- Pressure to hit growth targets
- More complex cap table

### Scenario 3: Venture (Conservative)

- Wait 12-18 months, raise Series A with traction
- **Pros:** Higher valuation, proven metrics
- **Cons:** Slower overall growth, more conservative path

**Recommendation:** **Bootstrap through Month 3 (Product Hunt), then re-evaluate funding.**
