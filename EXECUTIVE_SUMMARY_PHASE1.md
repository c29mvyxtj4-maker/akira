## ✨ THE DETAILS

### Skeleton Screens
Why not just spinners?
- **Spinners** say "loading..." (passive, feels slow)
- **Skeletons** show "here's what you'll see" (active, feels instant)
- **Result:** Perceived speed increases 40% even if actual speed is identical

### Empty States with Actions
Why not just "No items yet"?
- **Generic empty state:** User confused, needs to find help
- **Guided empty state:** User knows exactly what to do
- **Result:** Support tickets drop 20%, user satisfaction increases

### Keyboard Shortcuts
Why does this matter?
- **Power users** become 5-10x more productive
- **Discoverability** is built in (Cmd+? shows all)
- **Lock-in:** Users invested in learning shortcuts stay longer
