## SEND STRATEGY

### Optimal Send Times
| Prospect Type | Best Time | Why |
|---------------|-----------|-----|
| **Agencies** | Tuesday–Thursday, 9–10 AM | After standup, before inbox chaos |
| **Freelancers** | Tuesday–Wednesday, 6–7 PM | Evening work session |
| **Both** | Wednesday 10 AM | Highest open rates across both |

**Timezone:** Send in recipient's local time if possible (use HubSpot, Mailchimp, or manual segmentation)

### Follow-Up Sequence

**Day 1:** Send initial email (A, B, or C based on prospect type)

**Day 3:** Follow-up email (different subject line)
```
Subject: Quick follow-up: [NAME], still interested in AKIRA beta?
Body: [Short version of original email + direct link]
Word count: ~80 words
Tone: Casual, no pressure
```

**Day 7:** Final email (urgency)
```
Subject: Last chance: beta closes tomorrow (spots almost full)

Word count: ~50 words
Tone: Urgent but friendly
```

**Day 14:** Remove from sequence (don't over-pursue)

## CONVERSION TIPS

### Why These Templates Work

**Email A (Problem → Solution)**
- ✓ Opens with pain they feel (tool fatigue)
- ✓ Shows you understand their world
- ✓ Positions AKIRA as relief, not add-on
- ✓ **Expected open rate:** 35–45%
- ✓ **Expected click rate:** 12–18%

**Email B (Exclusivity)**
- ✓ Leverages FOMO (only 10 spots)
- ✓ Adds status (founding customer, early access)
- ✓ Clear benefits + small ask (feedback)
- ✓ **Expected open rate:** 40–50%
- ✓ **Expected click rate:** 15–22%

**Email C (Founder Story)**
- ✓ Authentic, vulnerable, relatable
- ✓ Founder voice = higher trust
- ✓ Low pressure (5 min, no card)
- ✓ **Expected open rate:** 38–48%
- ✓ **Expected click rate:** 14–20%

### A/B Testing Recommendation

**Test 1: Subject Line**
- A: Problem-focused (`"you're using 5 apps..."`)
- B: Exclusivity-focused (`"Selecting 10 beta customers..."`)
- **Expected winner:** B (FOMO usually wins)

**Test 2: CTA Button**
- A: "JOIN BETA LINK" (generic)
- B: "Claim Your Spot" (possessive, scarcity)
- **Expected winner:** B (+3–5% click rate)

**Test 3: Email Length**
- A: 150 words (short)
- B: 200 words (detailed)
- **Expected winner:** 180–200 words (sweet spot)

### Expected Response Rates

| Metric | Expected Range | Target |
|--------|---|---|
| **Open Rate** | 35–50% | 40%+ |
| **Click Rate** | 12–22% | 15%+ |
| **Signup Rate** | 30–50% of clicks | 40%+ |
| **Conversion Rate (Email → Signup)** | 4–10% | 6–8% |

**Example:** Send to 100 people → ~40 opens → ~6 clicks → ~2–3 signups = **2–3% overall conversion**

## CLOSING NOTES

### Send Order (By Prospect Type)
1. **Warm intros first** (highest conversion): Email C
2. **Cold outreach with high context** (referral source): Email A
3. **Inbound interest** (they visited site): Email B
4. **LinkedIn/Twitter cold**: Short version of A or B (~100 words)

### Do's & Don'ts

✅ **DO:**
- Personalize with their name and company
- Use their timezone for send time
- Follow up exactly on Day 3 & Day 7
- Test subject lines
- Track opens/clicks/signups

❌ **DON'T:**
- Send more than 1 email per day
- Spam if they don't respond after Day 7
- Use overhyped language ("SHOCKING," "EXPOSED")
- Add them to a newsletter without permission
- Hide the "join beta" CTA
