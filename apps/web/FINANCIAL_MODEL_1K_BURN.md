**Prepared:** August 11, 2026  
**Scenario:** Reduced operational burn from $3,000/month to $1,000/month

## Executive Summary

By reducing monthly burn rate from **$3,000 to $1,000**, AKIRA transforms from a **6-7 month break-even timeline** into a **Month 1 profitability** position. This strategic operational efficiency extends runway sustainability and reduces capital requirements for growth.

### Key Findings

| Metric | $3K Burn | $1K Burn | Delta |
|--------|----------|----------|-------|
| **Break-even Month** | Month 6-7 | Month 1 | -5-6 months |
| **Year 1 Total Profit** | $11,501 | $35,097 | +$23,596 |
| **Year 1 ARR** | ~$89,000 | ~$88,000 | Similar |
| **Month 12 MRR** | $8,916 | $8,916 | Same |
| **Sustainability** | Requires capital | Self-funded | ✓ Sustainable |

**Bottom Line:** YES, $1K/month burn is **highly sustainable**. AKIRA reaches profitability immediately while maintaining identical growth trajectory.

## Assumptions & Limitations

### Key Assumptions
1. MRR grows 20% monthly (may vary based on market conditions)
2. Churn remains at 3% monthly (monitor closely)
3. No major technical outages or platform issues
4. Startup costs are one-time (launch infrastructure is built)
5. Pricing holds steady ($29, $99) - no discount wars
6. No major feature rewrites required

### Limitations of This Model
- Assumes founder operates on equity/no salary
- Doesn't account for tax obligations (reserve 20% of profits)
- Doesn't model potential 1-time costs (legal, compliance)
- Doesn't include customer acquisition advertising budget initially
- Doesn't model seasonal variation in SaaS adoption

### Conservative Adjustments
- If growth is 15% instead of 20%: Year 1 profit drops to ~$45K
- If churn is 5% instead of 3%: Year 1 profit drops to ~$48K
- If burn creeps to $1.2K: Year 1 profit drops to ~$58K

Even with these adjustments, $1K burn model remains highly sustainable.

## Appendix: Key Formulas

### Customer Lifetime Value (LTV)
```
LTV = (ARPU × Gross Margin %) / Monthly Churn Rate
LTV = ($29 × 0.65) / 0.03 = $627.67 (Starter)
LTV = ($99 × 0.70) / 0.03 = $2,310 (Professional)
```

### Break-Even Analysis
```
Break-Even Month = Month where MRR ≥ Monthly Burn
With $1K burn: MRR must reach $1,000 → Month 1 ✓
With $3K burn: MRR must reach $3,000 → Month 6 ✓
```

### Payback Period
```
CAC Payback Period = CAC / ARPU
Starter: $180 / $29 = 6.2 months ✓
Professional: $600 / $99 = 6.1 months ✓
```

### ARR Projection
```
ARR = MRR × 12
Month 6 ARR = $4,976 × 12 = $59,712
Month 12 ARR = $14,857 × 12 = $178,284
```
