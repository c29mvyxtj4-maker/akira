## Who Is Our ICP? (Deep Dive)

### Company Profile
**"Digital Marketing Agency"**
- Offers services: social media management, content creation, campaign strategy, PPC, email marketing
- Works with B2B or B2C clients (restaurants, e-commerce, SaaS, local services)
- Revenue model: Monthly retainers ($1k-10k/month per client) + project fees

### Team Composition
- **Founder(s)**: 1-2 people (handles business dev + some delivery)
- **Creatives/Specialists**: 1-3 people (designers, copywriters, social media managers)
- **Optional**: Account manager/operations person

### Pain Points (Why They Need AKIRA)

1. **Tool Sprawl** (PRIMARY)
   - Uses HubSpot ($50-120) + FreshBooks ($15) + Harvest ($12) + Monday.com ($50) + Slack ($6.67)
   - Switches between 5+ apps daily to check client status, invoice, log hours
   - Data is scattered — "Did I invoice this yet?" requires checking 2 systems
   - Monthly tool bill: $133-250

2. **Client Management Chaos** (SECONDARY)
   - Clients ask "where's my project?" — have to check Monday or email threads
   - No single source of truth for client communication/history
   - Hard to see all projects across all clients at once
   - Onboarding new client = create contacts in HubSpot + task in Monday + invoice template in FreshBooks

3. **Invoice Pain** (TERTIARY)
   - Manual invoicing is tedious (copy-paste from previous invoice)
   - Hard to track who paid what (pending invoices scattered in email or FreshBooks)
   - Clients ask "did you receive my payment?" — have to dig through emails
   - No automatic reminders for overdue invoices

4. **Time Tracking Friction** (TERTIARY)
   - Team forgets to log hours until end of week (if at all)
   - Logging hours = switch to Harvest, add entry, switch back
   - Hard to see "how much did Project X cost in hours vs what we charged"

5. **No Team Visibility** (SECONDARY)
   - Owner can't see what the team is working on today
   - No single dashboard showing: active clients, pending invoices, upcoming deadlines

### Psychographic Profile

**Mindset**:
- Pragmatic — wants solutions that work, not fancy features
- Tired of SaaS subscription fatigue ("another $50/month for what?")
- Values time over money (would pay for tool that saves 5 hours/week)
- Growth-minded (wants to scale without hiring 10 people)

**Frustration Level**: HIGH
- Actively complaining about tool sprawl to peers
- Willing to try new solution immediately if it solves the problem
- Not loyal to existing vendors (HubSpot, FreshBooks) — just using them because "that's what everyone uses"

## Buying Behavior

### Decision Process
1. **Awareness**: Hears about AKIRA from peer recommendation or sees landing page
2. **Consideration**: Tries free beta (wants proof it works before paying)
3. **Decision**: Converts after 1-2 week trial + positive experience
4. **Validation**: Asks 1-2 team members for feedback before committing

### Price Sensitivity
- Would pay $29-79/month without hesitation (saves $80-150/month in tool consolidation)
- $199 tier is "only if we're big enough" (5+ people)
- Prefers annual to monthly if discount >10% ("lock it in and forget it")

### Time to Purchase
- Fast (1-2 weeks from trial to paid) — high problem intensity
- No lengthy approval process (founder decides)
- Wants to start using immediately

---

## What They Value (in order)

1. **Simplicity** — "Just works without setup"
2. **Integration** — "Everything in one place"
3. **Price** — "Costs less than 5 separate tools"
4. **Support** — "Can talk to a human if I'm stuck"
5. **Features** — "Has what I need, not 1000 I don't use"

---

## What They DON'T Want

- Complex workflows (they're not enterprise)
- Steep learning curve (no time for training)
- Tons of customization (prefer defaults)
- Monthly retainer-based pricing (prefer per-seat)
- Vendor lock-in without easy export

## Channels to Reach Them

### High-Efficiency (Founder-Led Sales)
1. **Direct outreach** (LinkedIn)
   - Search: "Marketing agency", "Digital agency", location EU
   - Personalized message: "Saw you run a marketing agency, built a tool specifically for you"
   - Can close 5-10% of outreach (~1 customer per 20 emails)

2. **Peer networks** (most effective)
   - Ask existing beta users for referrals
   - 50% of revenue often comes from word-of-mouth for this segment

3. **Industry communities**
   - Slack groups for agency owners (e.g., Agency Owners, Marketing Leaders EU)
   - Facebook groups (e.g., "Marketing Agencies Discussion")
   - Reddit (r/marketing, r/agencylife)

### Medium-Efficiency (Scalable)
4. **Content marketing**
   - Blog: "How to manage 15 clients without 5 SaaS tools"
   - Guide: "Agency Tech Stack 2026" (position AKIRA as simplification)

5. **Paid ads** (later, when PMF confirmed)
   - Google Ads targeting "CRM for agencies", "invoicing software"
   - LinkedIn ads targeting agency owners

### Low-Efficiency (Don't do yet)
- AppSumo, ProductHunt (fine for visibility, not for high-quality users)
- Outbound partnerships (take too long to set up)
