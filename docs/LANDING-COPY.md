## FAQ SECTION

### Q1: Is AKIRA secure?
**A**: Yes. We use Supabase (enterprise-grade PostgreSQL) with SOC 2 compliance on roadmap. Your data is encrypted and isolated by default. Same security as HubSpot or FreshBooks.

### Q2: Can I import my data from HubSpot/FreshBooks?
**A**: For now, you'll need to manually migrate or we can help. We're building import tools for v1.1. For most agencies, starting fresh takes 30 minutes.

### Q3: What if you shut down? Will I lose my data?
**A**: Annual billing is prepaid and non-refundable (you won't lose money). You'll always be able to export your data as CSV. We're committed to AKIRA long-term, but transparency first.

### Q4: Do you have an API?
**A**: Yes, on Professional + Enterprise tiers. Build custom integrations or connect to Zapier.

### Q5: Can I add more users later?
**A**: Yes. Upgrade anytime. Extra users on Professional are included; on Enterprise you can add unlimited.

### Q6: Is there a free tier?
**A**: Yes, during beta (free for 3 months). After that, we launch with paid tiers. We may add a limited free tier in v1.1, but no promises.

### Q7: Do you have a Slack integration?
**A**: Zapier integration available on Professional/Enterprise (connect to any Slack bot). Direct Slack integration on roadmap for v1.2.

### Q8: How is AKIRA different from [Monday.com / HubSpot / FreshBooks]?
**A**: 
- Monday.com: We focus on simplicity + invoicing (Monday is overkill for agencies)
- HubSpot: We're 10x cheaper + easier to set up
- FreshBooks: We add CRM + projects (FreshBooks is just invoicing)
We're built specifically for marketing agencies, not trying to be everything to everyone.

## Notes for Marc

- Keep copy **short and scannable** (bullet points, bold key words)
- Use **specific numbers** ($29/month, 5 tools, 3x faster) — they work better than vague claims
- **Avoid jargon** (no "synergies", "leverage", "paradigm shift" 😂)
- **Focus on outcomes, not features** (not "time tracking module", but "know exactly how profitable each client is")
- **Be specific to ICP** (mention HubSpot, FreshBooks, Monday by name — agencies know these)
- **Use their language** — if they say "tool sprawl", use "tool sprawl"
