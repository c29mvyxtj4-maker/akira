# AKIRA Positioning Statement

**Effective Date**: 2026-08-01  
**Target Audience**: Marketing agencies (1-5 people, EU/ES)

---

## Primary Positioning (30 words)

### Core Statement
> **"AKIRA is the all-in-one workspace for marketing agencies to manage clients, projects, and money without juggling 5 SaaS tools."**

---

## Why This Positioning Works

### The Problem (Resonates with Marketing Agencies)
Marketing agencies today use 5+ SaaS tools:
- **CRM**: HubSpot ($50-120/month) for client management
- **Invoicing**: FreshBooks ($15/month) for billing
- **Time Tracking**: Harvest ($12/month) to log hours
- **Projects**: Monday.com ($50/month) for task management
- **Communication**: Slack ($6.67/month) for team chat

**Total**: ~$133-250/month for tools that don't talk to each other.

**Frustration**: 
- "Where did I log those hours for client X?"
- "Is this invoice paid or pending?"
- "I need to check 3 tools to know project status"
- "Another password to remember"

### Our Solution
One tool that handles:
1. **Clients** (CRM) — manage all relationships in one place
2. **Projects** (Kanban) — track deliverables and deadlines
3. **Money** (Invoicing) — create, send, track payments

**Benefit**: Save $1,500+/year on tools, 5+ hours/week switching between apps.

### Why "Workspace" Matters
- Not a "tool" (sounds limited, boring)
- A "workspace" (sounds powerful, integrated, professional)
- Like Notion or Slack — a place where work happens

### Why "Marketing Agencies" (Specific)
- Specific ICP = stronger positioning
- Agencies have unique pain (multiple clients, variable scope)
- Easier to market (can target agency communities, Slack groups, newsletters)
- Better conversion (message matches their reality)
